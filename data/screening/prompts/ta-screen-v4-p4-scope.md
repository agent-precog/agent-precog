# Pillar 4 addendum to ta-screen-v4 (2026-11-13)

Given to the screening model together with ta-screen-v4 for records from the Pillar 4 search.

- These records come from the Pillar 4 search (edge inference and real-time constraints). The pillar's scope is: running learned models on edge silicon under hard latency budgets, and the effect of wireless tail latency on time-critical decisions. The relevant criterion is (d), "edge or real-time inference under explicit latency constraints for safety decisions". Read (d) literally: BOTH parts must be plausible from the title and abstract. (1) An explicit latency constraint: a stated deadline, latency budget, worst-case or tail latency, jitter, a measured inference latency or frame rate reported against a real-time requirement, or timing-predictable or deadline-aware inference. (2) A safety decision: the inference feeds a safety-relevant action or monitor (collision avoidance, autonomous driving or flight, robot control, hazard or fault detection that triggers a response, medical alarms, multi-agent or multi-robot coordination), or the paper studies how latency, jitter or wireless delay affects such time-critical decisions.
- Exclude E1: edge or on-device inference with no safety decision (keyword spotting, recommendation, generic image classification, agriculture yield, smart-home convenience, LLM chat on phones), model compression or quantisation benchmarks reported only as speed or accuracy with no time-critical use, federated or on-device training, generic offloading or resource scheduling in edge networks with no learned safety decision, and hardware accelerator designs with no safety application. Use E4 only as the prompt defines it. Criteria (a), (b), (c) and (e) still apply if a record happens to meet one of them.
