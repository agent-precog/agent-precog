# ta-screen-v4, Pillar 1 scope addendum (maintainer ruling, 2026-11-13)

Applied on top of ta-screen-v4 to the 837 Pillar 1 records that passed the strict re-screen of unread title and abstract passes. The maintainer ruled that the interceptor or hazard detector must protect a learned or AI system, and that world-model and abstention papers found by this pillar are judged against the Pillar 2 and Pillar 3 scopes. The ruling applies from 2026-11-13 and is not retrospective. Text given to the screening model:

- These are Pillar 1 records (runtime safety interception and multi-agent hazard detection) that ALREADY passed a strict screen under ta-screen-v4. You are re-screening them under the maintainer's Pillar 1 scope ruling of 2026-11-13. A record passes under criterion (a) or (c) ONLY IF the system being guarded or analysed is a LEARNED or AI system: the interceptor, shield, safety filter, runtime monitor, guardrail or hazard detector protects a learned policy (reinforcement learning, imitation learning, a neural controller or neural planner), an LLM or foundation-model agent, or a multi-agent system whose agents are learned or LLM-driven. The safety mechanism itself need not be learned: a control barrier function or reachability filter wrapped around a learned policy passes, and so does a formal runtime monitor on an LLM agent.
- Exclude E1 under this ruling: classical control with hand-designed controllers and no learned component being guarded (control barrier functions, reference governors, reachability, model predictive control or potential fields for collision or deadlock avoidance of conventionally controlled robots, vehicles or platoons); observer-based or model-based attack and fault detection in platoons, power systems or conventional multi-agent control; formal verification of hand-written multi-agent protocols; human-factors studies with no learned system. If the title and abstract give no sign that a learned or AI system is being guarded, exclude E1. If a learned or AI system is plausibly the thing guarded but the abstract is thin, include.
- Criterion (b) records are judged against the Pillar 2 scope: include only if the contribution is a world model or latent prediction of FUTURE state over a graph or over multi-agent state. Masked or self-supervised representation learning on a static graph is E1. Multi-agent trajectory forecasting passes only where it learns a latent or world model of joint agent dynamics; a plain trajectory regressor is E1.
- Abstention and deferral records are judged against the Pillar 3 scope: include only if abstaining, rejecting or deferring is the MAIN contribution AND one of these holds: (i) hand-off to a human or safeguard, (ii) sequential or multi-step setting, (iii) multiple experts, (iv) safety-critical runtime gate, (v) foundational theory of the reject option. One-shot selective prediction with none of these is E4.
- Use E2, E3 and E7 as the prompt defines them. In the reason for an include, name the criterion letter and say what learned or AI system is guarded.
